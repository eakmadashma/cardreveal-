3. Match real life

Do this once so the on-screen card is exactly the size of the real one, in the spot where you hold it.

While performing: drag with one finger. Tap with two fingers to bring the card back to the middle (or, with the tap option on, hide it so the next phone tap makes it appear again). Hold two fingers for a second to exit.

Drag the four dots onto the corners of the object. For a coin, use the corners of an imaginary square that just touches it. The orange edge becomes the top, so tap Rotate to turn it.

Hold the real object flat against the screen where you normally hold it. Drag the outline and adjust the size until its edges match exactly.
